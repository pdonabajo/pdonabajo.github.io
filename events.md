

## Gathering People. Awakening Purpose. Advancing God's Kingdom.

The Frequency Live exists to create environments where people can encounter God, grow in spiritual maturity, discover purpose, and participate in Kingdom transformation.

Our events are designed to bring believers together through prayer, teaching, worship, discipleship, leadership development, and evangelistic outreach.

Whether online or in-person, every gathering is built around one conviction:

**The Excellency of God is the Final Authority.**

From prayer meetings and live broadcasts to future conferences and city-wide revival gatherings, our mission remains the same: to help people know God, experience His presence, and impact their generation.
