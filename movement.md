# THE MOVEMENT


## The Frequency Live

The Frequency Live is a Kingdom-centered movement founded by Prince David (P.D.) Onabajo in 2025 with a mandate to proclaim the Excellency of God as the Final Authority in every sphere of life.

We believe that humanity was created to live in alignment with God's voice, purpose, and authority. Yet in a world filled with competing voices, confusion, and spiritual noise, many have lost connection with the frequency of truth.

The Frequency Live exists to restore that connection.

Through prayer, biblical teaching, discipleship, leadership development, media outreach, and community engagement, we are committed to helping people hear God's voice, understand His purpose, and walk in obedience to His will.

This is more than a ministry.

It is a movement of believers committed to pursuing God's presence, demonstrating Kingdom excellence, and transforming society through the power of Jesus Christ.

---

## Our Doctrine

### The Excellency of God

At the heart of The Frequency Live is the doctrine of The Excellency of God.

This doctrine affirms that God is supreme in authority, wisdom, holiness, truth, glory, and power. Every individual, institution, culture, and nation ultimately exists under His sovereignty.

The Excellency of God calls believers to live with reverence for God, submission to His Word, and dedication to His purposes.

We believe that true transformation begins when people encounter the greatness of God and willingly place Him at the center of every area of life.

---

## Our Slogan

### Final Authority

The phrase "Final Authority" represents our unwavering conviction that God's Word remains the highest standard for truth, faith, morality, leadership, and life.

In an age of competing ideologies and shifting values, we declare that God's authority is not optional—it is final.

His truth does not change with culture.

His standards do not change with opinion.

His purposes do not change with time.

God remains the Final Authority.

## Our Community

### Frequency Nation

Frequency Nation is the community of believers connected to the vision of The Frequency Live.

We are a diverse family of worshippers, intercessors, leaders, entrepreneurs, professionals, students, creatives, and Kingdom-minded individuals united by a common pursuit: to know God deeply and make Him known boldly.

Frequency Nation exists to:

• Grow spiritually through prayer and discipleship.

• Build meaningful Kingdom relationships.

• Develop leaders who influence society.

• Advance revival in communities and nations.

• Demonstrate God's excellence in everyday life.

Together, we seek to become a generation that carries God's presence, reflects His character, and advances His Kingdom.
