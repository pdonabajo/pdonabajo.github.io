# PRINCE DAVID (P.D.) ONABAJO

## Founder, The Frequency Live

### Proclaiming The Excellency of God as the Final Authority

Prince David (P.D.) Onabajo is a Christian leader, teacher, revival advocate, media visionary, Information Security Professional, and Real Estate Investor dedicated to advancing the Kingdom of God through prayer, teaching, discipleship, and digital media.

Born in Ketu, Lagos State, Nigeria, and originally from Ijebu-Ishara, Ogun State, P.D. Onabajo was raised in a Christian home where his spiritual journey began at an early age. Under the guidance of his mother, he attended Sunday School from infancy and transitioned into children's church as he grew. These early experiences laid the foundation for a lifelong pursuit of God, truth, and Kingdom purpose.

Throughout his life, he has maintained a deep conviction that God must remain the highest authority in every sphere of human existence. This conviction would later become the foundation of a doctrine known as The Excellency of God.

## Ministry Journey

In 2009, P.D. Onabajo founded Potent Prayer Evangelical Church with a passion to see lives transformed through prayer, biblical teaching, and the power of the Holy Spirit.

Over the years, his ministry burden expanded beyond traditional church structures into a broader vision: reaching people through media, technology, leadership development, discipleship, and revival initiatives capable of impacting nations.

This vision eventually gave birth to The Frequency Live in 2025.

The Frequency Live is more than a ministry platform—it is a movement committed to broadcasting the voice of truth to a generation searching for meaning, direction, and spiritual awakening.

## The Excellency of God

At the center of P.D. Onabajo's message is a doctrine called The Excellency of God.

This doctrine teaches that God is supreme in glory, wisdom, authority, power, holiness, and truth. Every aspect of life must ultimately submit to His lordship.

The Excellency of God calls believers to:

• Live under God's authority.
• Pursue holiness and truth.
• Walk in Kingdom excellence.
• Develop Christlike character.
• Impact society through faith and leadership.
• Advance God's purposes on earth.

This doctrine serves as the theological foundation of The Frequency Live.

## Frequency Nation

The growing community surrounding The Frequency Live is known as Frequency Nation.

Frequency Nation is a gathering of believers, leaders, intercessors, entrepreneurs, creatives, and Kingdom-minded individuals committed to pursuing God's presence and advancing His purposes in every sphere of society.

The community exists to cultivate prayer, discipleship, leadership, revival, and transformational influence.

## Faith, Media and Technology

Uniquely positioned at the intersection of ministry and technology, P.D. Onabajo combines his experience as an Information Security Professional with his passion for Kingdom advancement.

He believes modern technology and digital media are among the most powerful tools available for evangelism, discipleship, education, and global revival.

Through podcasts, digital broadcasts, teaching resources, conferences, prayer gatherings, publications, and future global initiatives, The Frequency Live seeks to reach millions across Africa and the nations of the world.

## Vision for the Future

The long-term vision of P.D. Onabajo is to establish a global movement that raises spiritually mature believers who live under God's authority and influence every sector of society.

Through prayer gatherings, revival meetings, leadership development, media platforms, missions, and strategic partnerships, he seeks to see communities transformed by the power of God and the truth of His Word.
