# What Is The Excellency of God?

## Understanding the Supremacy of God

*"For from Him and through Him and for Him are all things. To Him be the glory forever. Amen."* — Romans 11:36

### Introduction

One of the greatest problems facing humanity is not ignorance of God's existence but ignorance of God's excellency.

Many people acknowledge God, attend church, pray occasionally, and even believe in Him. Yet they often fail to recognize who God truly is. They know about God but have not fully grasped His greatness, His majesty, His holiness, His authority, and His glory.

The foundational doctrine of The Frequency Live is known as **The Excellency of God**.

This doctrine teaches that God is supreme in glory, wisdom, holiness, authority, power, truth, and majesty. There is no being, institution, government, philosophy, culture, ideology, or system that exists above Him.

The Excellency of God is not merely an attribute of God—it is the sum total of His infinite greatness revealed in His nature, character, works, and authority.

To understand God rightly is to recognize His excellency.

To recognize His excellency is to submit to His authority.

To submit to His authority is to live for His glory.

---

# The Meaning of Excellency

The word *excellent* refers to something that surpasses all others in quality, greatness, value, and distinction.

When we speak about the Excellency of God, we are declaring that God possesses a greatness that exceeds every standard of comparison.

There is nothing equal to Him.

There is nothing greater than Him.

There is nothing outside His authority.

The Psalmist declared:

> "O LORD, our Lord, how excellent is Your name in all the earth, who have set Your glory above the heavens!"
>
> Psalm 8:1

God is not merely excellent among many excellent things.

God alone defines excellence.

Everything we consider glorious, beautiful, wise, powerful, or majestic derives its existence from Him.

---

# God's Excellency in His Glory

The first dimension of God's excellency is His glory.

Glory refers to the manifestation of God's greatness, beauty, splendor, and divine presence.

Throughout Scripture, God's glory distinguishes Him from every created being.

The heavens themselves testify of His greatness.

> "The heavens declare the glory of God; and the firmament shows His handiwork."
>
> Psalm 19:1

When Moses asked to see God's glory, God revealed Himself in a way that transformed Moses forever.

Exodus 33:18 records Moses' request:

> "Please, show me Your glory."

Every genuine encounter with God begins when a person sees something of His glory.

The problem with much of modern Christianity is not that people have seen too much of God—it is that they have seen too little.

A small view of God produces weak faith.

A great view of God produces worship.

---

# God's Excellency in His Holiness

One of the clearest revelations of God's excellency appears in Isaiah's vision.

Isaiah writes:

> "In the year that King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord sitting on a throne, high and lifted up, and the train of His robe filled the temple."
>
> Isaiah 6:1

Around God's throne stood seraphim crying:

> "Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of hosts; the whole earth is full of His glory!"
>
> Isaiah 6:3

Notice that the angels did not cry:

"Powerful, powerful, powerful."

Nor:

"Wise, wise, wise."

They cried:

"Holy, holy, holy."

Holiness is the perfection of God's character.

It means God is completely pure, completely righteous, and completely separated from sin.

When Isaiah encountered God's holiness, he immediately became aware of his own condition:

> "Woe is me, for I am undone! Because I am a man of unclean lips."
>
> Isaiah 6:5

The closer a person comes to God's excellency, the more they recognize their need for grace.

---

# God's Excellency in His Wisdom

Human wisdom is limited.

God's wisdom is infinite.

The greatest minds in history have only scratched the surface of understanding reality.

God understands everything completely.

Nothing surprises Him.

Nothing confuses Him.

Nothing catches Him off guard.

Paul marveled at God's wisdom when he wrote:

> "Oh, the depth of the riches both of the wisdom and knowledge of God! How unsearchable are His judgments and His ways past finding out!"
>
> Romans 11:33

God never learns.

God never improves.

God never discovers.

God has always possessed perfect knowledge.

His wisdom is part of His excellency.

---

# God's Excellency in His Power

God possesses unlimited power.

The Bible repeatedly reveals Him as Creator, Sustainer, and Ruler of all things.

Genesis begins with the simple declaration:

> "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."
>
> Genesis 1:1

Creation itself is evidence of divine excellency.

The stars, galaxies, oceans, mountains, seasons, and laws of nature all testify to His power.

Jeremiah declared:

> "Ah, Lord GOD! Behold, You have made the heavens and the earth by Your great power and outstretched arm. There is nothing too hard for You."
>
> Jeremiah 32:17

God's power is not merely impressive.

It is absolute.

Nothing is impossible for Him.

---

# God's Excellency in His Authority

The slogan of The Frequency Live is:

**Final Authority.**

This flows directly from the doctrine of The Excellency of God.

God's authority is supreme because His excellency is supreme.

Authority belongs to Him because everything belongs to Him.

Jesus declared:

> "All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth."
>
> Matthew 28:18

Governments possess temporary authority.

Leaders possess delegated authority.

Institutions possess limited authority.

But God's authority is eternal.

No court can overrule Him.

No nation can dethrone Him.

No philosophy can replace Him.

No generation can outgrow Him.

His authority is final because His excellency is unmatched.

---

# God's Excellency in Christ

The fullest revelation of God's excellency is found in Jesus Christ.

Paul writes:

> "He is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn over all creation."
>
> Colossians 1:15

He continues:

> "For by Him all things were created that are in heaven and that are on earth, visible and invisible."
>
> Colossians 1:16

And finally:

> "He is before all things, and in Him all things consist."
>
> Colossians 1:17

Jesus is not merely a teacher, prophet, or religious figure.

He is the perfect revelation of God's excellency.

To see Christ is to see God's wisdom.

To hear Christ is to hear God's truth.

To follow Christ is to submit to God's authority.

---

# Humanity's Greatest Problem

Humanity's greatest problem is not lack of technology.

It is not lack of education.

It is not lack of wealth.

Humanity's greatest problem is the rejection of God's excellency.

Romans 1 describes what happens when people refuse to honor God:

> "Although they knew God, they did not glorify Him as God."
>
> Romans 1:21

Whenever people lose sight of God's greatness, they elevate something else.

Some elevate wealth.

Some elevate politics.

Some elevate culture.

Some elevate science.

Some elevate personal desires.

Some elevate themselves.

Idolatry begins whenever something takes God's place.

Revival begins whenever God returns to His rightful place.

---

# The Excellency of God and Spiritual Awakening

Every major awakening in Scripture began with a renewed vision of God.

Moses encountered God's glory.

Isaiah encountered God's holiness.

Ezekiel encountered God's majesty.

John encountered Christ's glory on Patmos.

Whenever people truly see God, transformation follows.

The Church does not need a greater view of itself.

The Church needs a greater view of God.

Revival begins when believers once again become overwhelmed by His excellency.

# Conclusion

The Excellency of God is the recognition that God alone is supreme in glory, wisdom, holiness, power, authority, truth, and majesty.

Everything exists because of Him.

Everything is sustained through Him.

Everything ultimately belongs to Him.

As believers, our calling is not merely to know about God but to recognize His excellency, submit to His authority, reflect His character, and live for His glory.

The world is transformed when people encounter the greatness of God.

The Church is revived when believers rediscover the greatness of God.

The purpose of The Frequency Live is to proclaim this truth to our generation:

**God is Excellent Beyond Comparison.**

**God is the Final Authority.**

**God alone deserves the glory.**

*"For of Him and through Him and to Him are all things, to whom be glory forever. Amen."* — Romans 11:36
