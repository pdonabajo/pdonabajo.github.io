# The Foundational Teachings of The Frequency Live

## Understanding the Movement, Doctrine, Community, and Mission

*"For from Him and through Him and for Him are all things. To Him be the glory forever. Amen."* — Romans 11:36

### Introduction

We live in a generation filled with voices. Every day, people are confronted with competing ideas, philosophies, opinions, and beliefs. Technology has connected the world, but it has also amplified confusion. In the midst of all the noise, one question remains:

**Whose voice carries the highest authority?**

The Frequency Live was established to answer that question with clarity and conviction.

Founded in 2025 by Prince Davide (P.D.) Onabajo, The Frequency Live is a Kingdom-centered movement committed to proclaiming the Excellency of God as the Final Authority in every sphere of life. Through prayer, biblical teaching, discipleship, leadership development, media outreach, and community engagement, we seek to help people align their lives with God's purpose and truth.

This article serves as an introduction to the foundational teachings and vision that define The Frequency Live.

---

## What Is The Frequency Live?

The Frequency Live is more than a ministry platform.

It is a movement dedicated to helping people tune their hearts, minds, and lives to the voice of God.

The word *frequency* speaks of alignment. Just as a radio must be tuned to the correct frequency to receive a clear signal, humanity was created to live in alignment with God. Sin, pride, rebellion, and worldly distractions have caused many people to drift away from the voice of their Creator.

Jesus declared:

> "My sheep hear My voice, and I know them, and they follow Me."
>
> John 10:27

The mission of The Frequency Live is to help people hear God's voice through His Word, respond in obedience, and live according to His purpose.

We believe that true life, peace, purpose, and transformation are found when people live on God's frequency rather than the frequency of the world.

---

## Our Foundational Doctrine: The Excellency of God

At the center of The Frequency Live is a doctrine known as **The Excellency of God**.

This doctrine teaches that God is supreme in glory, wisdom, power, holiness, authority, truth, and majesty. Everything exists because of Him, through Him, and for Him.

The Bible consistently reveals God's greatness:

> "Yours, O LORD, is the greatness, the power and the glory, the victory and the majesty; for all that is in heaven and in earth is Yours."
>
> 1 Chronicles 29:11

The Excellency of God reminds believers that:

* God is not equal to anyone or anything.
* God's wisdom surpasses human understanding.
* God's authority exceeds every earthly authority.
* God's truth remains unchanged regardless of culture.
* God's glory is the ultimate purpose of creation.

Far too often, humanity attempts to place itself at the center of life. The doctrine of The Excellency of God calls us back to a God-centered worldview where everything begins and ends with Him.

As Isaiah witnessed the holiness and greatness of God, he declared:

> "Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of hosts; the whole earth is full of His glory."
>
> Isaiah 6:3

Every genuine revival begins with a renewed vision of God's excellency.

---

## Our Slogan: Final Authority

The slogan of The Frequency Live is simple:

**Final Authority**

These two words summarize our conviction that God alone possesses ultimate authority over all things.

Jesus said:

> "All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth."
>
> Matthew 28:18

In a world where truth is often treated as relative and opinions compete for influence, we affirm that God's Word remains the highest standard for faith, morality, leadership, purpose, and life.

The Final Authority means:

* God's Word is greater than human opinion.
* God's truth is greater than cultural trends.
* God's wisdom is greater than worldly philosophies.
* God's standards do not change with time.
* God's purposes remain eternal.

The question for every believer is not merely, "What do I think?" but rather, "What has God said?"

The Frequency Live exists to encourage believers to submit every area of life to God's authority and to build their lives upon His truth.

## Our Community: Frequency Nation

A movement becomes stronger when people unite around a common vision.

Frequency Nation is the community arm of The Frequency Live.

It is a growing family of believers, leaders, intercessors, entrepreneurs, professionals, students, creatives, and Kingdom-minded individuals who desire to pursue God wholeheartedly and influence the world around them.

The Apostle Peter described believers as:

> "A chosen generation, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, His own special people."
>
> 1 Peter 2:9

Frequency Nation exists to help believers:

* Grow spiritually.
* Build meaningful Kingdom relationships.
* Develop leadership capacity.
* Pursue revival and prayer.
* Advance the Gospel.
* Demonstrate Kingdom excellence in everyday life.

We believe that transformation happens best in community.

No one fulfills God's purpose alone.

Together, we learn, grow, pray, serve, and advance the Kingdom of God.

## The Mission of The Frequency Live

Our mission is:

**To proclaim the Excellency of God as the Final Authority through prayer, biblical teaching, discipleship, leadership development, media outreach, and Kingdom-centered community.**

The Great Commission remains at the heart of everything we do:

> "Go therefore and make disciples of all nations."
>
> Matthew 28:19

Whether through podcasts, prayer gatherings, publications, leadership training, digital media, or future revival events, our goal is to help people encounter God's truth and walk faithfully in His purpose.

## Our Vision

Our vision is:

**To build a global movement that raises spiritually mature believers who live under God's authority, demonstrate Kingdom excellence, transform communities, and influence nations for the glory of God.**

We believe God is raising a generation that will not merely attend church but will carry Kingdom influence into every sphere of society.

This includes:

* Ministry
* Government
* Education
* Business
* Technology
* Media
* Family
* Community Leadership

As believers live under God's authority, they become agents of transformation wherever God places them.

## What We Believe About Prayer

Prayer is one of the foundational pillars of The Frequency Live.

Jesus taught:

> "Men always ought to pray and not lose heart."
>
> Luke 18:1

Prayer is more than asking God for things.

Prayer is partnership with God.

Prayer aligns our hearts with His will, strengthens our faith, deepens our relationship with Him, and positions us to participate in His purposes.

Every major move of God throughout history has been preceded by people who prayed.

We believe revival begins when God's people return to prayer.
