# Why God's Authority Is Final

## The Lordship of God Over All Things

*"Forever, O LORD, Your word is settled in heaven."* — Psalm 119:89

### Introduction

Every society is built upon authority.

Homes have authority structures. Schools have authority structures. Businesses have authority structures. Nations have authority structures.

Without authority, there is confusion.

Without authority, there is disorder.

Without authority, there is chaos.

The question is not whether authority exists.

The question is: **Whose authority is supreme?**

The slogan of The Frequency Live is:

**Final Authority**

These words express our conviction that God's Word remains the highest standard for faith, truth, morality, leadership, justice, purpose, and human conduct.

In a world filled with competing voices, shifting ideologies, and changing cultural values, God's truth remains constant.

The Final Authority doctrine teaches that God alone possesses ultimate authority because He alone is the Creator, Sustainer, and Lord of all things.

The ultimate question of life is not:

*"What do I want?"*

Nor is it:

*"What does society say?"*

The ultimate question is:

**"What has God said?"**

---

# The Source of All Authority

Authority originates with God.

No human being created authority.

No government invented authority.

No institution established authority.

Authority belongs to God because creation belongs to God.

The Bible opens with these words:

> "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."
>
> Genesis 1:1

Because God created all things, He possesses rightful authority over all things.

The Creator has authority over His creation.

The Potter has authority over the clay.

The Builder has authority over the structure.

The Owner has authority over what belongs to Him.

Psalm 24:1 declares:

> "The earth is the LORD'S, and all its fullness, the world and those who dwell therein."

Everything belongs to God.

Therefore, ultimate authority belongs to God.

---

# God's Authority Revealed Through His Word

One of the primary ways God exercises His authority is through His Word.

The Scriptures are not merely religious literature.

They are the revealed Word of God.

Paul writes:

> "All Scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness."
>
> 2 Timothy 3:16

Because Scripture originates from God, it carries divine authority.

God's Word does not require human approval to become true.

It is true because God has spoken.

Human opinions change.

Scientific theories develop.

Political systems rise and fall.

Cultural values shift.

But God's Word remains unchanged.

Jesus declared:

> "Heaven and earth will pass away, but My words will by no means pass away."
>
> Matthew 24:35

The permanence of God's Word reflects the permanence of God's authority.

---

# Truth Is Established by God, Not Culture

One of the defining challenges of our generation is the belief that truth is subjective.

Many people argue that truth is determined by personal preference, social consensus, or cultural acceptance.

Scripture teaches otherwise.

Truth originates with God because God Himself is truth.

Jesus said:

> "I am the way, the truth, and the life."
>
> John 14:6

Truth is not created by society.

Truth is discovered through God's revelation.

Culture may change its standards.

God does not.

Society may redefine morality.

God does not.

Generations may abandon biblical principles.

God does not.

Malachi 3:6 declares:

> "For I am the LORD, I do not change."

Because God is unchanging, His truth remains unchanging.

The authority of God's Word stands above the opinions of every generation.

---

# The Lordship of Jesus Christ

The clearest declaration of God's authority is found in the Lordship of Jesus Christ.

After His resurrection, Jesus announced:

> "All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth."
>
> Matthew 28:18

Notice the scope of that statement.

Not some authority.

Not partial authority.

Not limited authority.

**All authority.**

His authority extends over:

* Nations
* Governments
* History
* Creation
* Spiritual powers
* Human destiny
* Life and death

Jesus is not merely Savior.

He is Lord.

Many desire Christ as Savior.

Fewer desire Him as Lord.

Yet biblical Christianity requires both.

To acknowledge Christ as Lord means submitting every area of life to His authority.

---

# God's Authority and Human Freedom

Some people view authority as restrictive.

Scripture presents a different picture.

God's authority is not designed to oppress humanity but to guide humanity into flourishing.

Jesus said:

> "If you abide in My word, you are My disciples indeed. And you shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free."
>
> John 8:31-32

True freedom is not the ability to do whatever we want.

True freedom is the ability to live according to God's design.

A fish is free in water.

A bird is free in the sky.

Human beings are most free when living under God's authority.

Disobedience promises freedom but often produces bondage.

Obedience may require surrender, but it produces life.

---

# God's Authority Over Leadership

Every human leader exercises delegated authority.

No king, president, pastor, employer, teacher, or parent possesses independent authority.

All legitimate authority ultimately comes from God.

Paul writes:

> "There is no authority except from God, and the authorities that exist are appointed by God."
>
> Romans 13:1

This truth has two implications.

First, leaders are accountable to God.

Second, authority should be exercised according to God's standards.

Whenever leaders reject God's authority, they misuse their own authority.

The Final Authority doctrine reminds us that every earthly authority operates beneath God's greater authority.

---

# God's Authority Across Generations

One of the most remarkable aspects of God's authority is its permanence.

Human governments change.

Empires rise and fall.

Cultures evolve.

Civilizations disappear.

Yet God's authority remains unchanged.

The Psalmist declared:

> "Forever, O LORD, Your word is settled in heaven."
>
> Psalm 119:89

What God declared true thousands of years ago remains true today.

What God commands remains relevant.

What God forbids remains harmful.

What God promises remains trustworthy.

God's standards are not outdated.

They are eternal.

---

# Living Under God's Government

The Final Authority doctrine is not merely theological.

It is practical.

Every believer must choose whether God's authority will govern their life.

Living under God's government means:

### Submitting to Scripture

Allowing God's Word to shape beliefs, values, and decisions.

### Obeying Christ

Following Jesus not only as Savior but also as Lord.

### Rejecting Competing Authorities

Refusing to place culture, politics, personal desires, or public opinion above God's truth.

### Pursuing Holiness

Aligning our lives with God's character and standards.

### Seeking God's Will

Making decisions based on what God has revealed rather than personal preference alone.

The question is not whether we will serve an authority.

The question is which authority we will serve.

---

# The Greatest Rebellion

At its core, sin is rebellion against God's authority.

From the Garden of Eden onward, humanity has repeatedly chosen self-rule over God's rule.

The serpent's temptation to Eve was essentially this:

*"You can determine truth for yourself."*

That temptation still exists today.

Modern culture often celebrates autonomy, self-definition, and personal truth.

The Gospel calls us back to divine authority.

The Christian life begins when we stop asking God to submit to our desires and begin submitting ourselves to His will.

---

# The Final Authority and the Future

One day every earthly authority will end.

Every government will pass away.

Every institution will fade.

Every human achievement will eventually disappear.

But God's Kingdom will endure forever.

Philippians 2:10-11 declares:

> "That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow... and every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord."

History is moving toward that moment.

The Lordship of Christ will be universally recognized.

The question is whether we acknowledge His authority willingly now or reluctantly later.

---

# Conclusion

The phrase **Final Authority** is more than a slogan.

It is a declaration.

It is the recognition that God alone possesses ultimate authority over creation, truth, morality, leadership, and human life.

God's Word supersedes human opinion.

Truth is established by God, not culture.

Obedience produces freedom.

God's standards remain relevant across generations.

Every believer is called to live under God's government.

In a world filled with uncertainty, there remains one unchanging foundation:

**God has spoken.**

His Word stands.

His truth endures.

His Kingdom advances.

His authority is final.

*"Forever, O LORD, Your word is settled in heaven."* — Psalm 119:89

**The Excellency of God.**

**Final Authority.**
