# Making Christ Known

## Understanding Evangelism as Kingdom Expansion

*"Go into all the world and preach the gospel to every creature."* — Mark 16:15

### Introduction

The message of Jesus Christ is the greatest message ever entrusted to humanity.

It is the message of salvation for the lost, hope for the broken, freedom for the captive, forgiveness for the sinner, and eternal life for all who believe.

Yet the Gospel was never intended to remain hidden within church walls.

It was given to be proclaimed.

Jesus did not command His followers to simply gather information about Him. He commanded them to make Him known.

At The Frequency Live, we believe evangelism is not the responsibility of a few gifted individuals. It is the calling of every believer.

Every Christian has been entrusted with the privilege and responsibility of sharing the good news of Jesus Christ with the world.

Evangelism is not merely about increasing church attendance.

It is about advancing the Kingdom of God.

It is about making Christ known among individuals, families, communities, cities, and nations.

---

# What Is Evangelism?

Evangelism is the proclamation of the Gospel of Jesus Christ through both words and actions.

The word Gospel means "good news."

The good news is that through the life, death, burial, and resurrection of Jesus Christ, humanity can be reconciled to God.

Paul summarized the Gospel clearly:

> "Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, and that He was buried, and that He rose again the third day according to the Scriptures."
>
> 1 Corinthians 15:3-4

Evangelism is the act of communicating this message so that others may come to faith in Christ.

It involves:

* Sharing God's truth.
* Calling people to repentance and faith.
* Demonstrating God's love.
* Making disciples.
* Helping others grow in their relationship with Jesus.

Evangelism is not merely transmitting information.

It is inviting people into reconciliation with God.

---

# The Great Commission

The foundation of Christian evangelism is found in the Great Commission.

Before ascending into heaven, Jesus instructed His followers:

> "Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit."
>
> Matthew 28:19

This command was not given only to the apostles.

It was given to the Church.

Every generation of believers inherits the responsibility of carrying the Gospel to the next generation.

The command contains two important elements:

### Go

The Gospel is meant to move.

Believers are sent into the world rather than isolated from it.

### Make Disciples

The goal is not merely conversions.

The goal is discipleship.

Jesus calls us to help people become mature followers of Christ.

Evangelism begins with proclamation but continues through discipleship.

---

# Why Evangelism Matters

Evangelism matters because people matter to God.

Jesus declared:

> "For the Son of Man has come to seek and to save that which was lost."
>
> Luke 19:10

God's heart has always been directed toward redemption.

Throughout Scripture, we see His desire to rescue, restore, and reconcile humanity.

Peter writes:

> "The Lord is not slack concerning His promise... not willing that any should perish but that all should come to repentance."
>
> 2 Peter 3:9

Evangelism is not driven by guilt.

It is driven by love.

We share the Gospel because God loves people and desires that they know Him.

Those who have experienced God's grace naturally desire others to experience that same grace.

---

# The Message We Proclaim

The heart of evangelism is the Gospel itself.

The message is not self-improvement.

The message is not religious performance.

The message is not human achievement.

The message is Jesus Christ.

The Gospel declares:

* Humanity has sinned against God.
* Sin separates people from God.
* Jesus Christ died for our sins.
* Jesus rose from the dead.
* Salvation is available through faith in Him.
* Eternal life is found in Christ alone.

Jesus said:

> "I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through Me."
>
> John 14:6

The power of evangelism is not found in human persuasion.

The power is found in the Gospel itself.

Paul declared:

> "For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ, for it is the power of God to salvation for everyone who believes."
>
> Romans 1:16

---

# Evangelism Through Words

Faith requires hearing the message of Christ.

Paul asks:

> "How shall they believe in Him of whom they have not heard?"
>
> Romans 10:14

For this reason, believers must be willing to speak.

Evangelism includes:

* Personal conversations
* Public preaching
* Teaching
* Writing
* Media outreach
* Digital ministry
* Testimonies

The Gospel must be communicated clearly.

People cannot respond to a message they have never heard.

Sharing Christ requires courage, compassion, and faithfulness.

---

# Evangelism Through Actions

While evangelism includes proclamation, it also includes demonstration.

Jesus not only preached the Kingdom.

He demonstrated the Kingdom through love, compassion, service, and mercy.

Believers are called to reflect Christ through their actions.

Jesus taught:

> "Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father in heaven."
>
> Matthew 5:16

People should encounter the character of Christ through:

* Acts of kindness
* Compassion for the vulnerable
* Integrity in leadership
* Excellence in work
* Service within communities
* Genuine love for others

Good works do not replace the Gospel.

They support and strengthen its witness.

---

# Evangelism and Community Transformation

The Gospel changes individuals.

Changed individuals influence communities.

Throughout history, wherever the Gospel has taken root, communities have experienced transformation.

Families have been restored.

Education has advanced.

Hospitals have been established.

Injustice has been challenged.

Hope has emerged.

Jesus described His followers as:

> "The salt of the earth."
>
> Matthew 5:13

Salt preserves and influences.

Likewise, believers are called to influence society through Kingdom values.

Evangelism is not only about preparing people for eternity.

It also impacts life here and now.

---

# Evangelism Produces Disciples

The Great Commission does not end with conversion.

Jesus commanded His followers to make disciples.

A disciple is someone who learns from Christ, follows Christ, and helps others follow Christ.

Discipleship involves:

* Teaching Scripture
* Developing spiritual maturity
* Building Christian character
* Encouraging obedience
* Equipping future leaders

Healthy evangelism produces healthy disciples.

Healthy disciples become effective witnesses.

The Gospel spreads through disciples who make disciples.

---

# Raising Future Leaders

One of the long-term goals of evangelism is leadership development.

Throughout Scripture, Jesus invested in people who would continue His mission.

He trained disciples who later transformed the world.

Paul mentored Timothy.

Moses prepared Joshua.

Elijah prepared Elisha.

Kingdom expansion requires leadership multiplication.

Evangelism introduces people to Christ.

Discipleship develops them.

Leadership training equips them for Kingdom service.

The future strength of the Church depends upon raising faithful leaders who will continue proclaiming the Gospel.

---

# Empowered by the Holy Spirit

Evangelism cannot be accomplished through human effort alone.

Jesus told His disciples:

> "You shall receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you; and you shall be witnesses to Me."
>
> Acts 1:8

The Holy Spirit empowers believers to:

* Share the Gospel boldly.
* Love people genuinely.
* Demonstrate spiritual fruit.
* Persevere through opposition.
* Point others to Christ.

Evangelism is ultimately God's work carried out through surrendered people.

The Holy Spirit remains the primary agent of transformation.
