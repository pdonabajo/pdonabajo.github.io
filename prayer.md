# PRAYER RESOURCES

## Equipping Believers for a Life of Prayer, Fasting and Spiritual Growth

Prayer has been at the heart of Prince Davide (P.D.) Onabajo's ministry journey since the founding of Potent Prayer Evangelical Church in 2009.

At The Frequency Live, we believe prayer is not simply a religious activity—it is a divine partnership with God that transforms individuals, families, communities, and nations.

The Prayer Resources Center exists to equip believers with practical tools, biblical guidance, and spiritual resources that strengthen their prayer lives and deepen their relationship with God.

Whether you are a new believer learning how to pray or a seasoned intercessor seeking greater effectiveness, these resources are designed to help you grow in intimacy with God and alignment with His purposes.
