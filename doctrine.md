

## The Foundational Teachings of The Frequency Live

The Doctrine Library serves as the theological foundation of The Frequency Live. It exists to help believers understand God's nature, discover His purpose, grow in spiritual maturity, and demonstrate Kingdom influence in every area of life.

At the center of every teaching is one unchanging conviction:

God is the Final Authority, and His Excellency must be recognized in all things.

---

# THE EXCELLENCY OF GOD

## Understanding the Supremacy of God

The Excellency of God is the foundational doctrine of The Frequency Live.

It teaches that God is supreme in glory, wisdom, holiness, authority, power, truth, and majesty. There is no being, institution, government, philosophy, or system that exists above Him.

The purpose of humanity is not merely to know about God but to recognize His excellency, submit to His authority, and reflect His character in every sphere of life.

When people lose sight of God's excellency, they elevate themselves, culture, wealth, politics, or personal desires above Him. Every spiritual awakening begins with a renewed vision of God's greatness.

The Excellency of God calls believers to worship deeply, obey fully, and live continually for His glory.

Key Scripture:
Isaiah 6:1–5
Romans 11:36
Colossians 1:16–18

---

# FINAL AUTHORITY

## The Lordship of God Over All Things

The phrase Final Authority expresses our conviction that God's Word remains the highest standard for faith, truth, morality, leadership, and human conduct.

In a world filled with competing opinions and changing values, God's truth remains constant.

The Final Authority doctrine teaches that:

• God's Word supersedes human opinion.
• Truth is established by God, not culture.
• Obedience produces freedom.
• God's standards remain relevant across generations.
• Every believer must live under God's government.

The ultimate question of life is not "What do I want?" but "What has God said?"

Key Scripture:
Psalm 119:89
Matthew 28:18
2 Timothy 3:16–17

---

# PRAYER

## Partnership With God

Prayer is not merely communication with God; it is participation in His purposes.

Prayer aligns the believer's heart with God's will and creates an atmosphere for divine intervention.

The Frequency Live believes prayer is essential because:

• Prayer builds intimacy with God.
• Prayer releases spiritual authority.
• Prayer transforms individuals and communities.
• Prayer prepares hearts for revival.
• Prayer advances God's Kingdom on earth.

Every great spiritual movement throughout history has been preceded by persistent prayer.

Prayer is not the preparation for the work.

Prayer is the work.

Key Scripture:
Jeremiah 33:3
Luke 18:1
1 Thessalonians 5:17

---

# REVIVAL

## The Awakening of God's People

Revival is the restoration of spiritual passion, holiness, obedience, and hunger for God's presence.

Revival begins when believers recognize their need for God and respond with repentance, prayer, humility, and surrender.

Revival produces:

• Renewed devotion to Christ.
• Increased holiness.
• Passion for evangelism.
• Spiritual transformation.
• Community impact.

True revival is not measured by crowds but by changed lives.

The goal of revival is not emotional excitement but sustained obedience to God.

Key Scripture:
2 Chronicles 7:14
Habakkuk 3:2
Psalm 85:6

---

# EVANGELISM

## Making Christ Known

Evangelism is the responsibility of every believer.

The message of Jesus Christ is the hope of the world and must be proclaimed through both words and actions.

The Frequency Live believes evangelism involves:

• Proclaiming the Gospel clearly.
• Demonstrating Christ's love.
• Serving communities.
• Making disciples.
• Raising future leaders.

Evangelism is not merely church growth.

It is Kingdom expansion.

Every believer is called to become a witness of God's grace and truth.

Key Scripture:
Matthew 28:19–20
Mark 16:15
Acts 1:8

---

# LEADERSHIP

## Influence Through Service

Biblical leadership begins with submission to God.

The greatest leaders are not those who seek power but those who faithfully serve God's purposes and people.

Kingdom leadership is built upon:

• Character before charisma.
• Service before status.
• Integrity before influence.
• Responsibility before recognition.

Leadership is stewardship.

Every leader will ultimately give account to God for how they used their influence.

Key Scripture:
Mark 10:42–45
1 Timothy 3:1–7
Proverbs 11:14

---

# HOLINESS

## Reflecting the Character of God

Holiness is not legalism.

Holiness is becoming increasingly like Christ.

God calls believers to be distinct from the world while remaining actively engaged in it.

Holiness involves:

• Purity of heart.
• Obedience to God's Word.
• Integrity in private and public life.
• Separation from sin.
• Devotion to God's purposes.

Holiness is not achieved through human effort alone but through the transforming work of the Holy Spirit.

Key Scripture:
1 Peter 1:15–16
Hebrews 12:14
Romans 12:1–2

---

# KINGDOM CULTURE

## Bringing Heaven's Values to Earth

Kingdom Culture is the practical expression of God's rule in everyday life.

It reflects the values, priorities, attitudes, and character of Heaven within families, workplaces, communities, and nations.

Kingdom Culture promotes:

• Excellence.
• Integrity.
• Compassion.
• Stewardship.
• Justice.
• Humility.
• Service.
• Truth.

Believers are called not merely to attend church but to transform culture by living according to God's standards.

Kingdom Culture exists wherever God's authority is honored and His values are practiced.

Key Scripture:
Matthew 6:10
Romans 14:17
Matthew 5:13–16
