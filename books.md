The Books & Publications ministry of The Frequency Live exists to document, preserve, and distribute biblical teachings that help believers grow spiritually, develop Kingdom influence, and understand the Excellency of God.

# FEATURED BOOK

## The Excellency of God

### Understanding the Supremacy, Glory and Authority of God

The Excellency of God is the foundational work of Prince Davide (P.D.) Onabajo and serves as the theological cornerstone of The Frequency Live.

This work explores the greatness of God and His supreme authority over all creation, culture, institutions, and human life.

The book challenges believers to move beyond a casual understanding of God and embrace a deeper revelation of His holiness, wisdom, majesty, and power.

Topics explored include:

• The Nature of God's Excellency

• The Glory of God

• Divine Authority

• The Lordship of Christ

• Human Purpose and Destiny

• Kingdom Responsibility

• Worship and Reverence

• Living Under God's Government

This publication establishes the doctrinal framework upon which The Frequency Live is built.
